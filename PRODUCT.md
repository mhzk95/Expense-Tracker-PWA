# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
People seeking a personal hub to manage their productivity. They use this in a personal context to track expenses, keep journals, save passwords, and store research links.

## Product Purpose
A productivity application serving as a centralized space to save and manage all personal information. Success is efficient task completion and easy information retrieval.

## Positioning
A single, focused space for all your personal things.

## Brand Personality
Functional, Organized, Accessible.
Reference feel: Notion, Linear, Google Calendar (strong information hierarchy, consistent spacing, minimal distractions, easy scanning, clean typography, predictable interactions).

## Anti-references
- Dribbble-style showcases or heavily decorative UIs.
- Cluttered, trend-driven interfaces (excessive gradients, glassmorphism, neumorphism, heavy animations, oversized cards).
- Interfaces that hide important information behind unnecessary interactions.
- Social media-style feeds where visual content competes for attention.

## Design Principles
- Prioritize usability over visual appeal.
- Focus on clarity, consistency, and efficient task completion.
- Practice strong information hierarchy and minimal distractions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA alignment. Requires sufficient color contrast, clear typography, adequate touch targets, dynamic text scaling, and intuitive navigation. Do not rely on color alone for meaning. Animations must be subtle and purposeful with full reduced motion support.
